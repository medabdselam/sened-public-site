Setup

Three steps, with no server and no monthly subscription

  1. 01Install Sened on the restaurant's deviceThat device becomes the data centre: orders, items, inventory and reports all live there.
  2. 02Connect the waiter and kitchen devicesThey reach the restaurant's device over the local network you set up, not over the internet.
  3. 03Turn on backupA local copy is enough to start; add a Drive copy whenever you want protection beyond the device.
Google Drive permissions

What Sened asks of your account, and why

Linking is optional and is requested only if you decide to turn on cloud backup. Sened then asks for the narrowest scope that does the job — nothing more.

ScopeWhat it allowsWhy Sened needs it
drive.file Access only to files Sened created, or that the user opened through it Upload a database backup, list previous backups, download a selected backup to restore
Reading your other Drive files — Not needed, and not requested
Google data for other purposes — Never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred, never used to train models

What you should know: a backup file holds the restaurant's full data. Protect the Google account with a strong password and two-step verification, and never share backup links. You can disconnect from the app's settings at any time.

FAQ

What restaurant owners ask

Do I need internet to run the restaurant?

No. Daily operation runs on the local network. Internet is needed only to upload a backup to Google Drive or restore one.

Do I need a Google account?

No. A Google account is needed only for a backup in the cloud. Without it, backups stay local.

Where is the restaurant's data stored?

In a database on the device you control. Sened does not send your restaurant's data to a server of ours or to any third party.

What happens if the device fails?

Data is restored from the most recent backup: local, or from Drive if linking is on. Test a restore from time to time — an untested backup is not a backup.

How do I delete my data?

The data is on your device and in your own Drive account, and you can delete it yourself at any time. For any privacy request, write to our support address.
